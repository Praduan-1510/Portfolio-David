Three things your device will ask for

Here is what each one is actually used for, so you can decide before the prompt appears.

Notifications

Appointment reminders, dose reminders, and a nudge when a report is ready. Nothing about your health ever appears on a lock screen — only that something is waiting.

Location

To find the nearest pharmacy that has your medicine in stock, and the nearest open emergency room. Used only while the app is open.

Camera

To photograph a paper prescription, an insurance card, or a rash you want a dermatologist to look at.

Decline any of them and the app still works — you will just type a few things by hand.
