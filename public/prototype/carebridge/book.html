Biography

Dr Calloway has looked after hearts in central Texas since 2002. He trained in interventional cardiology at Brookline Community Hospital and now runs the cardiac catheterisation laboratory at Cedar Park Medical Center, where he places around three hundred stents a year. Most of his week, though, is ordinary clinic work: chest pain that turns out to be reflux, blood pressure that will not settle, people who feel breathless on the stairs and are frightened about what it means. He is known for drawing the problem on paper before he explains it, for saying plainly when a test is not needed, and for writing to your general doctor the same day rather than a fortnight later. He teaches the cardiology rotation for resident physicians and sits on the hospital's chest-pain pathway committee.
