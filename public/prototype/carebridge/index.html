About this prototype. Every person, facility and phone number comes from Appendix E of the specification — one shared cast so a screenshot in a deck matches a fixture in a test. Everyone here is fictional. Phone numbers use the 555 exchange, and while 911 is shown on the emergency surface it is never a live link: a click-through demo dialling a real emergency service is a genuine incident. No photographs are attached to any patient or clinician; avatars are generated initials on a deterministic tint.

It adapts to what it is opened on. There is no device picker. The app measures its own environment and lays itself out accordingly — a fixed 88 px rail and twelve-column field on a Mac or desktop, an eight-column layout with a floating tab bar on iPad, and a single column on iPhone. Resize the window and watch it hand off.
