Your sports. Your world.

A sports-retail app for India. One set of numbers runs through all 45 screens: rupees, real GST rates, and filter counts that add up.

Things worth trying

  1. Search Running shoes and open Filters: by type (22 + 12 + 8 + 6) and by brand, it all adds up to 48.
  2. In the ₹28,000 bag, RUN15 is refused (no running gear) and DECA10 takes ₹1,000 off.
  3. Check out in three steps. Pay with a new card ending 0000: it’s declined, and your bag survives.
  4. Turn 1,000 of your 2,450 Club points into a ₹100 voucher, then use its code in the bag.
