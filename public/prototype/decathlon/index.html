45 screens, six flows

13 original frames were rebuilt from the designs pixel for pixel, except Checkout, which became the confirm step of a three-step flow. 32 extended screens use the same components to finish every journey. Pick any screen to open it in the phone above.

Design system

Sky blue, navy, one warm accent

Light surfaces keep product photos in front. Brand blue marks what you can tap, navy carries the main actions, and peach marks where you are.

Gear up front

Sport tiles and product photos fill the screen. Prices, ratings and the heart sit on the card, so you can compare without opening every product.

Checkout in one hand

Size, bag, promo, address and payment all sit within thumb reach. The bag count stays on the tab bar, and a failed payment keeps your bag.

Local and upfront

Prices are in rupees, delivery dates follow your pincode, and stock is shown per store. Returns show the date and the refund before you confirm.
