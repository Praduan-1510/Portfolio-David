How auto-pay decides

Rails, in plain language

The auto-pay rules

Four rules stand between your account and any bill. All four must clear before a payment goes out without you.

Pattern
At least two prior matching charges from the same vendor before a bill is auto-payable.
Ceiling
$500 per bill by default — never above your setting.
Deviation
A charge more than 15% off its history is treated as a brand-new decision, whatever the amount.
First payments
A vendor’s first payment is always yours — a permanent rail, not a setting.
