A hull rides three ruled ledger lines, its keel — the V of the hull itself — dipping through a break in the top rule. The rules read twice: they are the waterline and they are ledger paper. The two unbroken rules beneath the keel are the even keel made literal, and the accountant’s double rule under a closed total.

One color
Ships as ink on paper (currentColor). On dark surfaces: cream on ink, as in the favicon tile.
Two-tone
Teal rules permitted on special surfaces only; the hull stays ink.
Clearspace
One rule-height (3 units of the 48 grid) on all sides, minimum.
Never
No recoloring beyond ink / teal / cream, no rotation, no strokes, no soft corners, no drop shadows.

Every color in the system, its job, and its measured contrast
Token Hex Role Contrast on paper
--paper #F6EFE0 App background — warm aged cream. Surface — carries ink text
--card #FDF9EE Cards and table surfaces — a lighter sheet laid on the paper. Surface — carries ink text
--ink #2B2416 Primary text, borders, and rules — warm near-black ink. 13.42:1
--ink-soft #6C6047 Secondary text and captions. 5.39:1
--teal #3E6E70 Primary actions, links, and filled meter ticks. 5.00:1 — the same reading for cream text on a teal fill, since contrast is symmetric
--teal-deep #2E5456 Teal hover state; teal used as text on paper. 7.28:1
--green #3D6B47 Credits, AUTO stamps, and success states. 5.41:1
--red #9E3B2F Debits, NEEDS APPROVAL, and the forecast floor line. 5.88:1
--amber-text #7A5410 Flag and warning text. 5.91:1
--amber-wash #F3E3BC Flag wash and highlight, always behind amber text. Wash — never a text color
--rule-dark #B8A87F Table rules and stronger dividers. Decorative only — never text

Debits are red and credits are green, but color is never the only signal — every amount carries its sign, and every state carries a stamped word.

Scale and rules

The type scale runs 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 21 / 28 / 36 / 52 pixels. Body text sits at a line-height of 1.65. Capital letters appear only ever in Courier — Merriweather is never letter-spaced, never set in caps — and every number, date, and amount is set in Courier as well.
