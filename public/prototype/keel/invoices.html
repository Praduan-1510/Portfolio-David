How reminders escalate

Automatic The first reminder sends on its own once an invoice crosses 7+ days overdue, in your standard friendly tone.
Always yours Every reminder after the first, or any firmer tone, requires your approval before it goes anywhere.
Dispute = full stop A disputed invoice pauses the entire sequence and routes to Approvals — Keel never keeps escalating on schedule into a dispute.
