Autonomy · How much Keel decides without you

You set the leash.

Tighten or loosen what Keel handles on its own. Two categories are permanently locked to Always Ask — those are safety rails built into the product, not settings you’ve left conservative.

Fixed behavior

Invoice reminders

The first reminder sends automatically once an invoice is 7+ days overdue, in your standard friendly tone. Every reminder after the first — or any firmer tone — comes to you for approval. If a client disputes an invoice, the whole sequence pauses and routes to Approvals.

Auto at 7+ days

Timing isn’t configurable; escalation is always your call.

Permanently locked · Safety rails

New vendor payments

The first payment to any vendor Keel hasn’t paid before always comes to you — regardless of amount, confidence, or any setting on this page. This is a rail, not a limitation: it’s what makes every other automation on this page safe to allow.

Always ask

Locked by design. No override exists.

Disputes & anomalies

Duplicate charges, disputed invoices, and out-of-pattern activity always stop and wait for a human. Confirming a flagged pair as legitimate de-prioritizes that exact vendor-and-amount match in future scans — it never blinds Keel to a genuine duplicate later.

Always ask

Locked by design. No override exists.

What Keel will never do

One-off payments
No net-new outbound payments beyond your established recurring bills — ever, at any setting.
Silent actions
Nothing happens without a logged, readable reason. Every entry in Activity opens to show its work.
Forecast meddling
A cash-flow flag never blocks or delays anything. It tells you; it doesn’t decide for you.
Tax advice
Keel keeps books; it doesn’t file or advise on taxes. That stays between you and your accountant.

Changing a threshold applies to new items only — anything already in the approval queue keeps the rules it was raised under.
