Identity

A mark for the zenith.

A meridian is the line the sun crosses at its highest point: the moment of peak clarity. The mark is an arc, a meridian line, and a zenith orb: a hemisphere brought to its brightest.

The App Icon

Rounded squircle, day-arc gradient, negative-space glyph. Legible from 16px favicon to a home-screen tile.
