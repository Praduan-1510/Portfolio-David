The project

From a spec with no signature: to a product you'd remember.

Meridian began as a 20,000-word functional specification for a B2B SaaS platform: the kind of document that defines everything the system does but, by its own admission, describes a design system with "no signature": correct, accessible, and indistinguishable from every competitor.

This project answers that gap. I designed a distinctive identity, the Day-Arc language, then built the platform out module by module as a working prototype: real interactions on mock data, a permissions engine, entitlement paywalls, an audit trail, and a lifecycle state machine. Not screens in a slideshow: an app you can actually use.

It spans the whole product surface: identity & tenancy, the core work surface, collaboration & search, billing & entitlements, operability, and a public API: all 18 modules, clickable end-to-end.

The identity

The Day-Arc language.

A meridian is the line the sun crosses at its highest point: the moment of peak clarity. That single idea drives the mark (an arc, a meridian line, a zenith orb), a warm-to-cool spectrum, and rounded, human type.
