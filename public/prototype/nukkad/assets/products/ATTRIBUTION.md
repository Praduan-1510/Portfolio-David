# Product art

All product images in this directory are original artwork generated by
`scripts/build-packshots.ts`. Nothing here is scraped, licensed or third-party.

Each pack is composed from a silhouette archetype chosen by shelf (carton, sack, tin, tube,
blister and so on), a palette approximated from the brand’s real trade dress, a gradient
face, an accent band and a printed label lockup. Backgrounds are transparent, so the same
asset sits correctly on paper white and on ink.

The colours are approximations, not reproductions: silhouettes are generic pack shapes and
no logo, wordmark or artwork belonging to any brand is reproduced. Colour alone is what
makes a shelf scannable, and that is all this borrows.

A photographic pipeline against Open Food Facts lives in `scripts/seed-catalog.ts` for a
real deployment, where product photography would come from brands or an in-house shoot.
