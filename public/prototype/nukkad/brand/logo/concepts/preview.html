Nukkad brand

Three marks, one decision

Each concept is shown the way it will actually be seen: as a 16px favicon, as an iOS 26 squircle, under Android's circular mask, in monochrome for themed icons, and sitting in an app drawer next to the competitors. Pick the one that still reads at 16px and still looks like itself in grey.

Concept 1

The Turn

A lowercase n drawn as one road that turns a corner. The right leg stops short and a dot completes it — the destination, just around the corner. The gap between them is half a stroke wide, and that gap is the last minute of the delivery. The arch's negative space is a doorway. Two shapes total, which is why it survives at 16px.

Concept 2

Shutter

The corner shop's rolling shutter, half up, warm light spilling out of the arch below — nukkad khula hai. It animates: on the splash screen the slats roll up and the arch grows to fill the frame. The most emotionally direct of the three, and the only one that says "a shop with a person in it" rather than "a logistics network".

Concept 3

Chowk

The crossroads seen from above: four blocks with a lane-width gutter between them, and the bottom-left block lit — that is the corner shop. It is the only concept that is also a system: the same four-square rhythm becomes map pins, category tiles and the loading grid. Geometric rather than illustrative, so it ages slowly.

The real test

In the app drawer

Every incumbent is a wordmark or a letter on a saturated field: Zepto violet, Blinkit yellow, Swiggy orange, Zomato red, BigBasket lime. Teal is the one saturated lane nobody occupies, and a geometric mark is the one shape nobody uses. This strip is where that claim is settled.
