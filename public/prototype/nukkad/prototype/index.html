Everything, a corner away

A quick-commerce prototype built as a direct answer to Zepto, Blinkit and Instamart — with the fee stack, the cancellation traps and the refund runaround designed out.

Things worth trying
  1. Add a few items and open the cart — one fee line, and the amount is on every button.
  2. Pay by UPI and watch the rider arrive. Speed it up from the demo panel.
  3. Turn on Reduce glass below: the whole chrome falls back to opaque surfaces.
  4. Switch to हिन्दी, or flip to dark.
