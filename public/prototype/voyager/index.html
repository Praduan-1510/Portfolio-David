45 screens, six journeys

24 original frames were rebuilt from the Figma designs and corrected so every date, price and total reconciles; 21 extended screens complete every journey with the same components. Select any screen to open it in the live phone.

Design system

Midnight navy, golden hour orange

A deep navy frame holds every screen like a passport cover. Orange marks the next step: prices, the primary action and progress. White sheets carry the content.
