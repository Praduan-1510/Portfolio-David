Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 Sep 2026

1. What we collect

Your name, email, phone number, date of birth and nationality; travel documents you choose to store; bookings, payment method tokens (never full card numbers) and the destinations you save.

2. Why we use it

To book and manage your trips, check you in with airlines and hotels, keep your documents ready at borders, prevent fraud, and — only if you allow it — send you deals for places you’ve saved.

3. Who we share it with

Airlines, hotels, transfer and tour partners that fulfil your booking; payment processors; and authorities when the law requires it (for example, advance passenger information). We never sell your data.

4. Travel documents

Passport and ID scans are encrypted on your device and in our vault. They’re shared with a partner only when you tap Share or check in online.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open, then up to 6 years for bookings where tax law requires. Deleting your account removes stored documents immediately.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time from Settings, or by writing to privacy@voyager.app. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

7. Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and optional analytics you can turn off in Email Updates and Notifications.
